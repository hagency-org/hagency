      --listen <URL>
          Transport endpoint URL. Supported values: `stdio://` (default), `unix://`, `unix://PATH`,
          `ws://IP:PORT`, `off`
          
          [default: stdio://]

      --stdio
          Use stdio as the transport (equivalent to `--listen stdio://`)
